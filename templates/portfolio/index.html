Featured Project • IoT & Django

AgroSense — Smart Farmer Crop Advisory System

Winner of ₹2,000 Cash Prize & Certificate at SSIT Tumakuru Hackathon

Problem Statement:

Traditional smallholder agriculture in developing regions relies on manual guesswork for crop irrigation. Overwatering drains water reservoirs and risks root disease, while under-watering stunts plant growth during critical heatwaves.

Integrated Solution:

An end-to-end smart agricultural advisory system pairing an ESP32 microcontroller in the field with a central Django web platform. Environmental sensors (Soil Moisture, DHT22 temperature/humidity, LDR light) continuously sample the field and broadcast telemetry over MQTT. The Django backend analyzes telemetry against crop moisture thresholds to automatically pulse water pump relays or notify the farmer via their dashboard.

Hardware & Software Stack:

Django Python ESP32 Microcontroller MQTT Protocol Soil Moisture Sensor DHT22 Temp & Humidity LDR Light Sensor Relay Water Pump
